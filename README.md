## 🚀 Featured projects

### 🧳 [YourSweep: why travellers pay, when, and who goes annual](https://github.com/ioannamalli-hub/yoursweep-sql-portfolio)
**SQL · SQLite · Python · matplotlib**: funnel, retention and plan-choice analysis for a subscription travel safety app (7-day trial, monthly or annual). Built with CTEs, recursive CTEs, window functions and self-joins on app-store exports. *Public version runs on synthetic data with the same shape as the real exports.*

- Apple turns downloads into payers about **4x** better than Google Play, and most payers skip the free trial.
- About **half** of monthly payers renew each month; Apple users who cancel almost always do it on **day 0**.
- Annual is chosen at the start or never, and an annual customer is worth about **5x** a monthly one over 12 months.
